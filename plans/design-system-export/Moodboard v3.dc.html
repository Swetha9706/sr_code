02 · Typography

Display & headers — Londrina Solid Aa Chunky, hand-drawn display face. Headlines, section headers, wordmark, stickers, big numbers. Never below 28px, never for paragraphs.
Body & UI — Bricolage Grotesque Aa Calm, legible grotesk with a little character. Carries case-study text, nav, buttons, forms. 400 / 500 / 700.

04 · Supporting elements

Torn & cut edges
Ragged clip-path edges on section transitions and photo frames. One torn edge per view.
Tape, stickers, stamps
new! SHIPPED ✓
Rotated ±3–10°. Tape on photos and paper scraps only, never on product screens. Round stickers mark status or calls to action.
Borders & shadow
1px cocoa outline + 2px hard offset for anything clickable. Hairline linen border for reading surfaces. No blurred shadows.
Pattern · square grid A faint raspberry-tint square grid, like graph paper or a design canvas. Use behind the hero and section intros, never under body text.
Shapes & radius
Radius 10 / 14 / 20 / 28 / full. Paper scraps are the only sharp-edged shapes.
Motion
Hover: lift 1px, shadow 2→3px, 160ms Collage pieces drift a few px with scroll (parallax) Stickers pop in with slight overshoot, once Case studies: fade-up only, no wobble Respect prefers-reduced-motion

05 · The collage dial

Yes: go loud on the homepage, strip back in case studies. The trick is not to switch styles but to turn one dial down. Same colours, type and outlines everywhere; only the amount of collage changes. That keeps the case studies recognisably yours while letting recruiters scan them quickly.

01 · The problem

Body copy sits at 18px Bricolage, 65–70 characters per line, on the Ivory surface. Headings switch to the grotesk at this depth so the page reads like a document. Londrina Solid is kept for the title and the occasional pull-out stat.

62% of new users dropped off before linking a bank account.

Collage appears once per case study at most: a single sticker on a key metric, or one taped process photo.

09 · Footer

The footer is the reward for scrolling, so it's the second loudest moment after the hero. Torn edge into cocoa, an oversized wordmark that bleeds off the bottom, one sticker, and plain links. Identical on every page, so case studies still end with personality.
